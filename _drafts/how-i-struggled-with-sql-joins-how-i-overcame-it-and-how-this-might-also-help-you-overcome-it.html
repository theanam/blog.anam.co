tl;dr: I'm not very comfortable correlating between a ven diagram and database tables.

I struggled with SQL. It was one lof those things I really really wanted to learn but just could not understand it. Yes I could do some occasional select, insert or maybe updated. Some subquery here and there but that was it. And to be honest, at some point of my life, I thought this is the best I would ever be with SQL. I used to look at the fancy queries my coworkers would make, and was in most cases, dumbfounded.

My then engineering lead once said, if something can be done with subqueries, they can also be done with joins. With a few times efficiency. This made me more interested to learn about joins.
