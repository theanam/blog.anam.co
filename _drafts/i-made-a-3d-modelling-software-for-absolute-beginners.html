It might look very similar. Because it is. And I would be lying if I did not mention that it's heavily inspired by Tinkercad. The way I think of it in my mind is a Free and open source alternative of Tinkercad.

So, how did I end up making it? Let's start from the beginning: I got my first 3D printed around 2020. It was an Anycubic Mega S. And I was hooked. I did not know almost anything about the world of 3d back then. But 3D printing taught me a lot. And eventually I wanted to design my own 3D models to print. And was looking for the easiest way to do it. then I found Tinkercad. It was amazing. Within hours I could make useful 3D models for my own use.

Tinkercad is good. For basic designs it's very good. But then I started to grow out of it. I wanted to make my designs at least semi parametric, it did not have that feature. It's free but then autodesk keeps changing how you login and licensing around the product. Some day I feel like I might wake up one day and lose all my designs there.

The idea of a parametric easy to use cad software had been in my mind since 2021. I did not have the expertise or understanding to make ie happen. But throughout the years I have learned how application like these work, the user experience and the mechanics. And then with the help of Gen AI, I could finally see myself actually making it.

Introducing: babycad:

In short: Babycad is a fun 3D modelling software. If you are new to 3d modelling it's the perfect starting point. You can combine basic shapes to create complex geometry.
